# Method review — conditional Fog influence diagnostic

Reviewed `/tmp/sherlock-fog-influence-diagnostic.md` read-only against the replay-buffer and inference source. No models/Torch, tests, games, or repository edits. Freeze preserved.

## Concrete review

- **The `ns` + `next_mask` pairing is correct.** `agents/sherlock/replay_buffer.py:31–40` stores `ns` with the supplied `next_mask` in the same row; `:52–76` saves/loads them as `ns` and `mask`. `agents/sherlock/main.py:1550–1574` obtains next-state features and masks together, and terminal/dead rows get `done=True`; excluding `done != 0` and requiring at least one legal action is the right filter. Do not pair `s` with this mask. One nuance: the stored mask is the agent's *computed legal mask* for that next state, not the mask reconstructed from `ns` alone; that is acceptable and is why the paired stored mask should be used.
- **The legal proposal comparison matches the relevant inference path if it uses ensemble Q, then the stored mask, then argmax.** `agents/sherlock/main.py:338–349` averages the principal Q and extra Q members, masks illegal actions, then takes the greedy legal action. Candidate construction/configuration is fixed in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-08-fog-comparison.md`. Preserve the comparison's exact member ordering and outer config. If a loaded checkpoint contains a CNN policy, the plan's member-Q arithmetic alone does not reproduce full inference (`main.py:346–347` calls `_mix_cnn` before masking); verify the accepted V14 archive/config has no active CNN policy or include that mixing for the full-policy comparison. This does not invalidate the explicitly neural-proposal-only metric.
- **Sampling has a small ambiguity, not a blocking flaw.** “Distinct rows” guarantees no replacement within each arm, but the plan does not require deduplication across arms. Paired trajectories can share many exact `(ns,next_mask)` rows, so “10,000 states” may be fewer unique feature/mask pairs and its row count must not be presented as an independent sample size. Persisting selected row indices and source hashes is good. Add a unique-pair count (and optionally report per-buffer counts); do not make inferential intervals from these rows.
- **This cannot test decision quality or Fog attribution.** Replay files contain only `s, ns, a, r, done, mask` (`replay_buffer.py:77–78`), no explicit final Fog-origin field. The planned shared pool is trajectory-dependent and trained-on. As already stated in the plan, it can show whether trained pairs alter legal neural proposals and margin contributions on this fixed pooled buffer; it cannot say the changed action is better, that Fog contexts are responsible, or that downstream rules/final actions preserve the change. Avoid implying “rule override rate” unless the experiment separately records/replays those rules; feature rows plus masks are insufficient to run the complete stateful decision pipeline.
- **Member contribution arithmetic should define the comparator precisely.** For each state/action pair, report `mean(new_pair Q) - mean(original36 Q)` as its direct mean contribution, and separately the change in the full mean `2/38 * (mean(new_pair)-mean(original36))`; report both at the V14 winner and candidate winner. The first is not the pair's contribution to the final 38-member ensemble. Float32 forward/mean reproduction check is appropriate; tolerate only a predeclared numerical roundoff bound.

## Does this reduce uncertainty without extending the failed wave?

**Yes, narrowly, if run only after the fixed comparison is fully decided and accepted.** It can resolve whether these trained pairs measurably move V14's *legal neural proposals* on their shared replay-state distribution, and whether changes are suppressed by the 38-member mean. It costs no games and does not extend the failed/active performance wave. It cannot establish reward causality, policy quality, final-action influence, or generalization; it is not a substitute for or reason to rerun the fixed comparison. Given the active comparison and no new mechanism question, it is optional and should not run until its decision is complete.
