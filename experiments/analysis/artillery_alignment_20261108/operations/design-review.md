## Actual callchain and smallest change

It calls `param_est.observe` before `_act`, then `remember` with the actual emitted world-coordinate action array. ParamEstimator remembers a copy and subtracts actual move/SAP costs at the next observation; `dropoff(default=0.0)` is a read-only confidence-gated posterior query. Therefore **keep this callchain unchanged**. Both experimental arms already require `rules_in_training_all=True`, so both estimate parameters. Enabling only the new flag without that existing estimator guard must not read stale state as though estimation were active.

It then calls `_artillery_ok`; only after a true return does it check `art_still`.

Only the existing `not training` guard currently permits estimated splash to lower the hit threshold and relax safety. Thus confident .5 lowers hits but retains safety; confident 1 lowers hits and removes safety; unknown/.25 preserves the old rules. Energy/reserve checks remain identical.

## Main causal limits to say explicitly

1192 helper calls/24 differences on one of two selected old losses establish reachability only. New standalone training visits a different state distribution than the36-net evaluation policy. A helper delta is neither the executed class nor a quality judgment; replay labels reflect rules, not raw neural preference. Changed SAPs also alter later estimator evidence, enemy response, survival, training masks and replay sampling; those are consequences of the intervention, not a separately isolated splash-quality effect. More estimated hits/reward or lower loss is not higher win rate. Two training starts do not support population-wide causal certainty. Closed HOLD/fog misses neither prove this change will fail nor justify loosening its prospective gates. V14 remains until the new fixed candidate actually clears release evidence.
