## Limits, not defects

Twenty screen maps and40 confirmation maps have limited power and coarse estimates. Direct point score≥50% is only a screen; confirmation's lower-bound gate is stronger. Failure/ambiguity means no qualified successor, not proof of no possible benefit. Balanced sides are not independent extra map samples. Bootstrap bounds can be unstable or degenerate at ceiling rates;40/40 or80/80 never establishes universal100% wins.

Unadjusted bootstrap intervals are descriptive approximations, not exact simultaneous coverage or a guarantee. Selecting between two fixed candidates does not itself invalidate testing the selected agent on genuinely fresh data. Requiring all release conditions is a conservative operational rule; it does not eliminate bootstrap approximation limits or justify claims outside the tested panel.

NEUTRAL−CONTROL remains a useful preplanned paired screen analysis. Two training starts and this small block cannot establish a general causal reward advantage.
